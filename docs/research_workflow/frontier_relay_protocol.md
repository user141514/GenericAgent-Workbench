# Frontier Relay Protocol

Frontier relay is the handoff at the end of a research turn. It keeps the next
agent or human from restarting from vague context.

## Handoff Fields

- Inspect next: files, logs, papers, benchmarks, or traces to examine.
- Test next: the smallest experiment or diagnostic to run.
- Exclude next: paths made unlikely by current evidence.
- Claim status: what claim remains possible if the idea fails.
- Open question: the one uncertainty that blocks the next decision.

## Quality Bar

A good relay should make the next turn executable in minutes. It should not end
with generic advice such as "do more experiments" unless it names the exact
experiment and the decision rule.
